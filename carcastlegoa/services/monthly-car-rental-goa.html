The Smart Solution for Extended Stays in Goa

Goa has evolved into India's premier hub for digital nomads, tech entrepreneurs, creative professionals, and remote workers. Buying a car or relying on daily taxi rentals is neither cost-effective nor practical for stays lasting 1 to 6 months.

Car Castle Goa's Monthly Car Subscription delivers the freedom of vehicle ownership without the depreciation, insurance premiums, registration red tape, or maintenance headaches. With a single predictable monthly payment, you get a fully serviced, insured car delivered directly to your villa or apartment in Assagao, Siolim, Porvorim, or Candolim.

Monthly Rental Benefits with Car Castle

  • Substantial Cost Savings: Enjoy daily effective rates as low as ₹930/day compared to standard ₹1,500 daily rates.
  • Complimentary Routine Servicing: Every 5,000 km or 30 days, we perform scheduled oil and brake inspections or provide a temporary doorstep replacement vehicle so your life never stops.
  • Commercial Insurance Included: Full third-party and comprehensive accidental coverage protects you from unexpected liability.

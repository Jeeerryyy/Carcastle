Seamless Airport Handover at Goa's Two International Airports

Goa is serviced by two major international airports separated by over 60 kilometers: Dabolim Airport (GOI) in South-Central Goa and Manohar International Airport, Mopa (GOX) in extreme North Goa. Depending on where your flight lands and where your hotel is located, taxi fares can easily exceed ₹2,500 to ₹4,500.

With Car Castle Goa, your rental car is waiting right in the airport pickup zone as you step out with your bags. Our airport logistics team tracks your flight status in real time, ensuring no delays even if your flight arrives early or late.

Dabolim Airport (GOI) Pickup Procedure

Located near Vasco da Gama, Dabolim Airport is convenient for travelers heading to Central Goa (Panjim, Dona Paula) and South Goa resorts (Colva, Cavelossim, Majorda, Palolem). Our staff coordinates via WhatsApp 30 minutes before your landing, shares the vehicle registration number, and guides you to the short-term parking lot just 100 meters from the domestic arrival gate.

Mopa Airport (GOX) Pickup Procedure

Situated in Pernem, North Goa, Mopa Airport is closest to Northern beach hubs like Morjim, Mandrem, Arambol, Vagator, and Anjuna. Once you exit arrivals, our executive meets you at the designated pickup bay. Inspection and digital handover take less than 5 minutes, allowing you to hit the new Mopa Expressway without delay.

Goa Airport Car Rental Guide: Dabolim (GOI) vs Mopa (GOX) Pickup Tips

By Car Castle Travel Desk • Updated: October 2026 • 5 Min Read

Airport Selection Cheat Sheet

  • Mopa (GOX) - North Goa: Ideal for travelers staying in Arambol, Morjim, Mandrem, Vagator, Anjuna, Calangute, and Panjim. Direct elevated expressway connects to NH66.
  • Dabolim (GOI) - South Goa: Ideal for travelers staying in Vasco da Gama, Bogmalo, Majorda, Colva, Benaulim, Cavelossim, and Palolem.
  • Delivery Guarantee: Car Castle tracks your flight live. Even if your flight is delayed, your car and executive are waiting at the terminal.

Goa is serviced by two modern international airports: the historic Dabolim Airport (GOI) in South Goa and the state-of-the-art Manohar International Airport at Mopa (GOX) in extreme North Goa. Choosing the right airport delivery strategy guarantees a stress-free start to your holiday.

Arriving at Manohar International Airport Mopa (GOX)

Commissioned to serve the booming travel demand in North Goa, Mopa Airport is located in Pernem taluka. It features wide multi-lane arrival roads and dedicated commercial pickup parking zones.

  • Transit to Coastal North Goa: A dedicated 6-lane elevated expressway connects Mopa directly to NH66. Driving time to Morjim or Ashwem is approximately 35 minutes; to Calangute or Candolim is around 45 to 55 minutes.
  • Handover Procedure: Walk out through the main Arrivals Gate. Our executive will be stationed at the short-stay passenger pickup zone with the vehicle keys, clean car, and digital agreement ready on WhatsApp.
  • Dedicated page: Mopa Airport Car Rental Details.

Arriving at Dabolim Airport (GOI)

Located near Vasco da Gama, Dabolim Airport remains the most convenient gateway for South Goa luxury resorts and central Panjim visits.

  • Transit to South & Central Goa: Travel to Panjim is just 30 minutes via the NH66 New Zuari Bridge. South Goa beach resorts in Majorda and Cavelossim are reachable within 25 to 40 minutes.
  • Handover Procedure: Exit the domestic arrival concourse and walk across to the passenger pickup lane opposite Gate 2. Our delivery team assists you with luggage loading and vehicle inspection.
  • Dedicated page: Dabolim Airport Car Rental Details.

Cross-Airport Pickups and Drop-offs

Many travelers land at Mopa (GOX) to spend their first few days exploring North Goa's beach nightlife, then drive down to relax in South Goa before flying out of Dabolim (GOI). We facilitate seamless cross-airport return handovers. Simply notify us of your flight itinerary when reserving your car on WhatsApp.

Documents Required to Rent a Self-Drive Car in Goa (2026 Guide)

By Car Castle Travel Desk • Updated: October 2026 • 4 Min Read

Document Checklist Summary

  • Indian Citizens: Valid Indian Driving License (Original or DigiLocker) + Government ID proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID).
  • Foreign Tourists & NRIs: Original Country Driving License + International Driving Permit (IDP) + Valid Passport & Indian Tourist Visa.
  • Verification Time: Takes less than 3 minutes via WhatsApp before vehicle dispatch.

Renting a self-drive car in Goa is simple, rapid, and fully digital. To ensure compliance with the Goa Transport Department and insurance safety standards, every renter must provide valid identification and driving credentials before vehicle handover.

Document Checklist for Indian Citizens

  1. Valid Indian Driving License (LMV - Light Motor Vehicle): Must be non-expired. Learner's licenses (LL) are legally prohibited for commercial self-drive cars.
  2. Proof of Identity & Address: One official government ID card, such as Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID.
  3. Digital Acceptance: If you left your physical wallet behind, verified digital documents shown on DigiLocker or mParivahan are completely accepted.

Document Checklist for Foreign Nationals & NRIs

  1. Original Country Driving License: Issued in your home country (must be written in English or accompanied by an official English translation).
  2. International Driving Permit (IDP): Issued in your country of origin in accordance with the 1949 or 1968 Geneva/Vienna Road Traffic Conventions.
  3. Passport & Tourist Visa: Copy of your passport photo page and valid Indian tourist/business e-visa stamp.

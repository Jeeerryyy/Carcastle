Self-Drive vs Taxi in Goa: Complete Cost Comparison & Trip Guide

By Car Castle Travel Desk • Updated: October 2026 • 5 Min Read

Cost Verdict at a Glance

  • Airport Return Taxis: Mopa Airport to Calangute return: ₹4,500 – ₹5,500 total.
  • Self-Drive 4-Day Rental: Maruti Swift 4 days @ ₹1,500/day = ₹6,000 total (includes entire airport transit + all sightseeing).
  • Net Savings: You save over ₹6,000 to ₹12,000 on a 4-day vacation while enjoying total privacy and flexibility.

One of the first questions travelers face when booking a vacation in Goa is: Should we rely on local taxis or rent a self-drive car? Unlike other major Indian tourist states, Goa does not have ride-hailing services like Ola or Uber. Transportation decisions have a massive impact on both your vacation budget and your daily freedom.

The Reality of Goa Taxi Fares in 2026

Local tourist taxis operate from fixed union taxi stands outside hotels, railway stations, and airports. Because drivers do not use meters for daily tourist trips, fares are negotiated or based on high union tariff sheets:

  • Mopa Airport (GOX) to Calangute / Baga: ₹2,200 to ₹2,800 one-way.
  • Dabolim Airport (GOI) to Candolim / Vagator: ₹1,800 to ₹2,500 one-way.
  • Full-day sightseeing taxi (8 hours / 80 km limit): ₹3,500 to ₹5,000 per day. Any extra kilometer or waiting time past 8 hours incurs steep surcharges.
  • Late-night dinner trip (Calangute to Anjuna & back): ₹1,500 to ₹2,000.

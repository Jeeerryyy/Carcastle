Goa Parking and Toll Guide: Beach Parking Fees, Bridges & Rules

By Car Castle Travel Desk • Updated: October 2026 • 4 Min Read

Key Parking & Toll Facts

  • Bridge Tolls: Atal Setu and New Zuari Bridge are completely toll-free for passenger cars.
  • Beach Parking Fees: Village panchayats charge official parking fees of ₹50 to ₹100 per entry at major beach spots.
  • No-Parking Enforcement: Panjim, Calangute, and Margao police use wheel clamps on illegally parked vehicles with fines starting at ₹500.

Navigating parking and bridge tolls in Goa is straightforward once you understand local municipal conventions. Here is your handy reference guide to parking without stress.

Beach Parking Rates Across North and South Goa

At major beaches like Calangute, Baga, Candolim, and Colva, local village panchayats operate designated pay-and-park grounds:

  • Four-Wheelers (Cars/SUVs): Typically ₹50 for 4 hours, or ₹100 for full-day parking. Always ask for an official printed panchayat ticket.
  • Baga Tito's Lane Parking: Private parking grounds charge ₹100 to ₹150 during peak evening party hours.
  • Secluded Beaches (Morjim, Ashwem, Agonda): Ample roadside open ground parking is generally free or included when visiting beachfront shacks.

Parking in Panjim & Heritage Fontainhas

Panjim city enforces strict street parking rules. Avoid parking in residential bylanes in Fontainhas where lanes are narrow. Instead, utilize the Patto Multi-Level Car Parking facility or the pay-and-park bays along the Dayanand Bandodkar Marg waterfront. Fines for blocking traffic or parking in "No Parking" zones include wheel clamps and ₹500+ tow-away fees.

State Toll Plazas in Goa

Unlike some other states with frequent highway toll booths, Goa does not collect toll fees on its major intra-state bridges: both the Atal Setu over the Mandovi and the Zuari Bridge connecting North and South Goa are 100% toll-free for four-wheelers. State entry tolls apply only to out-of-state commercial vehicles crossing borders from Maharashtra or Karnataka; our Goa-registered rental fleet is fully exempt from internal border checkpost fees.

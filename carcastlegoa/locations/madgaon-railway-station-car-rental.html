Direct Train Handover at Madgaon Railway Junction

Madgaon Junction (MAO) is Goa's busiest railway station, receiving superfast Rajdhani, Tejas, and Vande Bharat expresses from Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi, and Kerala. Station taxi queues are often chaotic with inflated pricing for South Goa beach hotels.

With Car Castle Goa, your rental car is waiting right in the station parking lot. As your train pulls in, simply step out, verify your digital agreement, load your luggage into your own boot, and drive straight to your beach cottage or resort.

Local Driving Tips for Madgaon Railway Station

How Self-Drive Rental Works in Goa

From booking to vehicle return — transparent rules, minimal documentation, and zero waiting time.

1. Documents Required

Renting a self-drive car with Car Castle Goa requires minimal documentation:

  • For Indian Citizens: Original valid permanent Driving License (Learner's License not permitted) + Aadhaar Card or Voter ID copy.
  • For International Visitors: Valid Driving License from your home country + International Driving Permit (IDP) + Passport copy with valid Indian Visa.
  • Age Requirement: Primary driver must be at least 21 years of age with a minimum of 1 year of driving experience.

2. Fuel Policy (Level-to-Level)

All vehicles operate on a fair Level-to-Level fuel policy. If your car is delivered with 50% fuel on the gauge, simply return it with 50% fuel. There are no mandatory fuel refill surcharge markups. Numerous petrol stations operate near Dabolim Airport, Mopa Airport, Panjim, and along NH66.

3. Security Deposit & Refund Timeline

A modest refundable security deposit ranging from ₹0 to ₹3,000 (depending on car model) is collected at handover. Upon vehicle return, our representative performs a quick exterior check and refunds your deposit immediately on the spot via UPI (Google Pay / PhonePe) or cash.

4. Insurance & Breakdown Support

Every car in our fleet is covered by commercial self-drive vehicle insurance. In the rare event of a mechanical breakdown, battery issue, or flat tyre anywhere in Goa, our 24/7 mobile roadside assistance team dispatches immediate support or provides a replacement vehicle free of cost.

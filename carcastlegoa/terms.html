Rental Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Car Castle Goa. By renting a self-drive vehicle from Car Castle Rentals Goa, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following operational terms and conditions:

1. Eligibility and Authorized Drivers

The renter must be at least 21 years old and hold a valid, original Driving License (Indian or International Driving Permit) along with a government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card or Passport). Only individuals whose credentials have been recorded during handover are authorized to operate the vehicle.

2. Permitted Territory & Boundary Restrictions

All Car Castle vehicles are registered with yellow-board commercial self-drive permits strictly for operation within the geographical boundaries of the State of Goa. Cross-border travel into neighboring states (Maharashtra or Karnataka) without prior written consent and interstate road permit payment is strictly prohibited.

3. Speed Limits & Traffic Compliance

Goa Police strictly monitors traffic violations using automated speed cameras and highway patrols. The maximum speed limit on Goa state highways (NH66 / NH566) is 80 km/h, and 40-50 km/h in town centers. Driving on beaches or sand dunes is an illegal criminal offense under Goa tourism law. The renter is solely responsible for any e-challans or traffic fines incurred during the rental tenure.

4. Fuel & Return Timing

Vehicles are provided on a Level-to-Level fuel policy. Billing is based on 24-hour cycles. Grace periods up to 1 hour are provided with advance notice; delays exceeding 1 hour are charged at ₹200/hour or the full day tariff.

5. Vehicle Damage & Insurance Deductibles

In the event of accidental damage, comprehensive insurance applies subject to standard insurance surveyor assessment. Minor scratches or dents caused by negligence are deducted from the security deposit at actual repair cost.
